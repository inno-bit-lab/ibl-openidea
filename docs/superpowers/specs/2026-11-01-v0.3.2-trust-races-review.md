## What the plans are about

MT-6 (PR #22) left two races in project trust that the v0.3.1 release notes list as known limitations. While planning, rev-claude found six interleavings, all confirmed in the source by rev-codex:

- a1: a grant concurrent with `close_project` reopens agent admission on a closed manager;
- a2: grant and revoke interleave, leaving store and project flag restricted while admission is open;
- a3: a revoke during `open_project` publishes a trusted project the store says is restricted (new finding, moved to the dependent plan);
- b1: `AgentManager::create` has no admission gate, so a task inserted after a revoke launches its agent;
- b2: a revoke inside `ensure_session`, before `live` is registered, kills nothing;
- b3: `probe` spawns unregistered and waits up to 30 s.
